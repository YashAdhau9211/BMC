## DETAILED BLOCK EXPLANATIONS

### 1. CUSTOMER SEGMENTS

**Who are we creating value for? Who are our most important customers?**

Prompt2Figma targets multiple customer segments in the design and development ecosystem, with a primary focus on UI/UX designers who need rapid prototyping capabilities. Our primary segment consists of professional UI/UX designers working in agencies, startups, or as freelancers who spend 60-70% of their time on repetitive wireframing tasks. These designers value speed, iteration capability, and the ability to quickly translate ideas into visual representations.

Our secondary segment includes product managers and non-technical stakeholders who need to communicate design concepts without deep design tool expertise. They benefit from natural language input that doesn't require mastering complex design software. The tertiary segment comprises frontend developers who need production-ready code from designs, reducing the design-to-development handoff friction.

The growth segment focuses on startups and small design agencies (5-50 employees) that lack extensive design resources but need rapid MVP development and client presentation capabilities. Finally, our future enterprise segment targets large organizations requiring self-hosted solutions, advanced security features, and custom integrations with existing design systems. This multi-segment approach allows us to capture value across the entire design-to-development workflow while maintaining focus on our core designer audience.

---

### 2. VALUE PROPOSITIONS

**What value do we deliver to the customer? Which customer problems are we solving?**

Prompt2Figma delivers transformative value by solving the fundamental design-to-development gap that costs organizations thousands of hours annually. Our primary value proposition is 95% faster design creation - what traditionally takes 2-4 hours of manual wireframing now takes less than 3 seconds. This dramatic time reduction allows designers to focus on creative problem-solving rather than repetitive component placement.

Our iterative editing capability with full context preservation sets us apart from competitors. Unlike single-shot AI design tools, Prompt2Figma maintains complete design history and context across unlimited iterations, enabling conversational design refinement like "add a search bar" or "make it more minimalist" without losing previous work. This addresses the critical problem of context loss in traditional design workflows.

The production-ready React code generation with AST validation ensures that exported code is syntactically correct and immediately usable, eliminating the typical 3-5 hours developers spend translating designs into code. Our comprehensive version control system tracks every design iteration with metadata, solving the problem of design evolution tracking that plagues collaborative teams.

For enterprises, we offer open-source flexibility and self-hosted deployment options, addressing security and compliance concerns that prevent many organizations from adopting cloud-based AI tools. The built-in security features (rate limiting, input sanitization, session management) and performance monitoring provide enterprise-grade reliability that justifies premium pricing tiers.

### 3. CHANNELS

**Through which channels do our customer segments want to be reached? How are we reaching them now?**

Our primary distribution channel is the Figma Plugin Marketplace, which provides direct access to our target audience of 4+ million Figma users worldwide. This marketplace offers built-in discovery, trust through Figma's vetting process, and seamless installation. We complement this with our GitHub repository, which serves both as a distribution channel for self-hosted deployments and as a credibility signal to technical audiences, showcasing our open-source commitment and code quality.

Our product website and landing page serve as the central hub for education, conversion, and support, featuring interactive demos, video tutorials, and case studies that demonstrate ROI. We leverage content marketing through technical blog posts, design workflow tutorials, and AI integration guides to establish thought leadership and drive organic traffic. This content is distributed across design community forums (Designer News, Dribbble) and developer communities (Dev.to, Hacker News) to reach both segments.

Social media channels, particularly Twitter and LinkedIn, enable us to engage with design and development communities, share user success stories, and announce feature updates. Email marketing campaigns nurture leads through the conversion funnel with onboarding sequences, feature highlights, and upgrade prompts. Partnership channels with design agencies, bootcamps, and educational institutions provide bulk licensing opportunities and brand credibility. This multi-channel approach ensures we reach customers at various touchpoints throughout their decision journey while maintaining consistent messaging about speed, quality, and ease of use.

### 4. CUSTOMER RELATIONSHIPS

**What type of relationship does each customer segment expect us to establish? How costly are they?**

Our customer relationship strategy is tiered to match the expectations and value of each segment while maintaining cost efficiency. For free and pro tier users, we employ a self-service platform model where users can independently discover, install, and use the plugin without human intervention. This scalable approach is supported by comprehensive documentation, video tutorials, and an interactive onboarding flow that guides users through their first design generation in under 2 minutes.

Community support forms the backbone of our relationship strategy for individual users. We maintain active forums, a Discord server, and GitHub discussions where users can ask questions, share tips, and showcase their creations. This community-driven support model reduces our direct support costs while building user engagement and loyalty. Power users often become community advocates, answering questions and creating tutorials, which amplifies our reach organically.

For team tier customers, we provide email support with 24-hour response times, ensuring that small agencies and startups receive timely assistance without requiring dedicated account management. This strikes a balance between personalized service and operational efficiency. Enterprise customers receive dedicated account managers who understand their specific workflows, provide custom integration support, and conduct quarterly business reviews to ensure maximum value realization.

We also invest in automated relationship management through in-app messaging, personalized feature recommendations based on usage patterns, and proactive outreach when users haven't engaged recently. This data-driven approach allows us to maintain relationships at scale while identifying opportunities for upselling and preventing churn. The total cost of customer relationships scales from near-zero for free users to approximately 15% of contract value for enterprise customers.

### 5. REVENUE STREAMS

**For what value are our customers willing to pay? How would they prefer to pay? How much does each revenue stream contribute?**

Our primary revenue stream is a freemium subscription model designed to convert users progressively as they realize value. The free tier (50 sessions/month) serves as a powerful acquisition tool, allowing users to experience the core value proposition without financial commitment. Based on industry benchmarks, we project a 5-8% conversion rate from free to paid tiers, with users typically converting after 2-3 weeks of regular use when they hit session limits.

The Pro tier ($29/month or $290/year) targets individual designers and freelancers who need unlimited sessions, priority processing, and advanced features like code generation. This tier is projected to contribute 60% of total revenue in years 1-2, with an average customer lifetime value of $870 (30-month retention). The Team tier ($99/month for 5 users) addresses small agencies and design teams, offering collaboration features, shared session history, and centralized billing. This tier provides higher revenue per customer ($1,188/year) with longer retention (42 months average) due to organizational switching costs.

The Enterprise tier (starting at $499/month) offers custom pricing based on user count, self-hosted deployment options, SSO integration, and SLA guarantees. Enterprise contracts typically range from $6K-50K annually and are projected to contribute 30% of revenue by year 3. These customers value security, compliance, and custom integrations enough to pay premium prices.

Secondary revenue streams include API access licensing ($0.01 per request) for developers building on our platform, white-label solutions ($5K-50K per client) for agencies wanting branded versions, and training/consulting services ($150/hour) for enterprise onboarding. We also plan marketplace revenue from third-party plugins and design system templates (20% commission). This diversified revenue model reduces dependence on any single stream while maximizing customer lifetime value across segments.

### 6. KEY RESOURCES

**What key resources do our value propositions require? What resources are most important in our distribution channels?**

Our most critical resource is our AI/ML technology integration with Google Gemini 2.5 Flash, which powers the core wireframe and code generation capabilities. This partnership provides access to state-of-the-art language models without the massive capital investment required to train proprietary models. However, we've built significant intellectual property in our prompt engineering techniques, structured output schemas, and validation systems that ensure consistent, high-quality results from the AI models.

Our full-stack development team represents our second most valuable resource. The team's expertise spans TypeScript/Figma Plugin API (frontend), Python/FastAPI (backend), distributed systems (Celery/Redis), and AI integration. This rare combination of skills is difficult to replicate and provides a significant competitive moat. The team's knowledge of both design workflows and software architecture enables us to build features that truly solve designer pain points rather than just showcasing AI capabilities.

The proprietary codebase itself is a key resource, representing 11,200+ lines of production code with 85%+ test coverage. This includes our session management system, iterative editing engine with context preservation, AST validation pipeline, and security infrastructure. The code's quality and architecture enable rapid feature development and reliable scaling. Our Redis-based state management system is particularly valuable, providing sub-10ms latency for session retrieval while supporting 1,500+ concurrent users.

Cloud infrastructure (AWS) provides the scalability and reliability required for enterprise customers, while our brand and reputation in the design community drive organic growth and reduce customer acquisition costs. Finally, customer data and analytics inform product development, with usage patterns revealing which features drive engagement and conversion. This data becomes increasingly valuable as our user base grows, enabling more sophisticated personalization and predictive modeling.

### 7. KEY ACTIVITIES

**What key activities do our value propositions require? What activities are most important in our distribution channels?**

AI model integration and prompt engineering constitute our most critical ongoing activity. This involves continuously refining our prompts to improve wireframe quality, reduce hallucinations, and handle edge cases. We dedicate approximately 30% of engineering time to experimenting with different prompt structures, schema definitions, and validation rules. As AI models evolve, we must adapt our integration to leverage new capabilities while maintaining backward compatibility with existing sessions.

Plugin development and maintenance require constant attention to Figma's evolving API, user feedback, and bug fixes. We release updates bi-weekly, balancing new features with stability improvements. The plugin's rendering engine, which translates JSON wireframes into Figma nodes, requires ongoing optimization to handle complex layouts and maintain performance. We also invest heavily in error handling and graceful degradation to ensure users never lose work due to API failures or network issues.

Backend API development focuses on scalability, security, and new endpoint creation. As we add features like team collaboration, design system integration, and advanced analytics, the backend complexity grows. We maintain strict API versioning and backward compatibility to avoid breaking existing integrations. Quality assurance activities include automated testing (85%+ coverage), load testing (monthly), security audits (quarterly), and user acceptance testing for major features.

Customer support and community building activities are essential for retention and word-of-mouth growth. We allocate 20% of team time to answering questions, creating tutorials, and engaging with users on social media. Marketing and content creation activities include blog posts, case studies, video tutorials, and conference presentations that establish thought leadership. Finally, partnership development with design agencies, educational institutions, and technology platforms expands our reach and creates new revenue opportunities.

### 9. COST STRUCTURE

**What are the most important costs in our business model? Which key resources are most expensive? Which key activities are most expensive?**

Our cost structure is dominated by fixed costs in the early stages, with variable costs increasing as we scale. Development team salaries represent the largest fixed cost at approximately $200K annually for a lean team of 4 (2 full-stack engineers, 1 designer, 1 product manager). This assumes competitive market rates for senior talent with AI/ML and distributed systems expertise. As we grow, we'll need to expand to 8-10 team members by year 2, increasing this cost to $400K-500K annually.

Cloud infrastructure costs scale with usage but remain relatively modest due to our efficient architecture. At current scale (1,000 users), monthly AWS costs are approximately $225 (EC2 instances, ElastiCache, ALB, CloudWatch). At 10,000 users, this increases to $450-600/month, and at 100,000 users, approximately $1,200-1,500/month with CDN integration. These costs are highly predictable and scale linearly with user growth, making them manageable through pricing strategy.

AI API costs (Google Gemini) represent a critical variable cost that scales with usage. At $0.0005 per request (average), 100K monthly requests cost $50. However, as we grow to 1M requests/month, this increases to $500, and at 10M requests/month, $5,000. We mitigate this through caching strategies, request optimization, and tiered pricing that passes costs to heavy users. Enterprise customers with high usage pay premium prices that cover their API costs plus margin.

Marketing and customer acquisition costs are projected at $30K annually initially, focusing on content marketing, community building, and targeted ads. As we scale, this increases to $100K-200K annually to maintain growth rates. Customer support costs scale with user base but are kept low through self-service documentation and community support. Payment processing fees (Stripe: 2.9% + $0.30) represent 3-4% of revenue. Office and operational costs ($50K/year) cover legal, accounting, insurance, and administrative expenses. Total year 1 costs are projected at $300K-400K, reaching break-even at approximately 1,200 paid users.

Prompt2Figma's business model is built on solving a real, expensive problem (design-to-development friction) with a technically sophisticated solution that delivers measurable ROI (95% time savings). The freemium model enables viral growth through the Figma marketplace while the tiered pricing captures value from different customer segments. Strategic partnerships with Google and Figma provide technology and distribution advantages that would be impossible to replicate independently.

The cost structure is lean and scalable, with break-even achievable at 1,200 paid users (projected month 8-10). Multiple revenue streams (subscriptions, API licensing, white-label, consulting) reduce risk and maximize customer lifetime value.

With proper execution, Prompt2Figma can capture 1% of the serviceable addressable market within 3 years, generating $2.4M in annual revenue while establishing itself as the leading AI-powered design-to-code platform.
